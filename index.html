A world of cooking traditions.

Explore 90 recipe drafts from 18 countries. Recipes, Metric and Imperial instructions, country introductions and an offline atlas are included in World Plate for iPhone and iPad.

Help us improve World Plate

Open TestFlight, select World Plate, then choose Send Beta Feedback. Tell us what happened, what you expected, and the version and build shown in Settings. A screenshot is helpful. Avoid including private information in screenshots.

What to try

Choose your default measurement system on first opening. Switch between Metric and Imperial at the top right of a recipe. Explore the country collections and the offline atlas, save favorites, and check them after reopening the app. Try sharing a recipe and exporting a local backup to storage you choose.

Recipe review is ongoing

The recipes are original research drafts and have not been kitchen-tested or dietary-reviewed. Dietary and allergy filters will be available after ingredient reviews. Check ingredients and product labels for your needs.

Privacy questions

Read the privacy policy here or offline in Settings. For questions or requests about feedback held by the developer, use TestFlight's Send Beta Feedback option.
