A world of cooking traditions.

Explore the offline country and regional recipe collections. Recipes, Metric and Imperial instructions, country introductions and an offline atlas are included in World Plate for iPhone and iPad.

Help us improve World Plate

Open TestFlight, select World Plate, then choose Send Beta Feedback. Tell us what happened, what you expected, and the version and build shown in Settings. A screenshot is helpful. Avoid including private information in screenshots.

What to try

Choose your default measurement system on first opening. Switch between Metric and Imperial at the top right of a recipe. Explore the country collections and the offline atlas, save favorites, and check them after reopening the app. My Kitchen saves pantry ingredients and an equipment checklist on your device. Its first 24 checked recipe guides compare listed ingredients and essential equipment; check recipe quantities and cookware sizes yourself. Food barcode lookup is optional, requires a network connection, and asks you to confirm the product and recipe ingredient. Try sharing a recipe and exporting an app-data backup to storage you choose.

Recipe review is ongoing

Recipes have not been kitchen-tested. Gluten Free and allergen filters include only recipes with the appropriate current ingredient checks; unreviewed and uncertain recipes are excluded from affected selections. Pantry or barcode entry does not verify allergens. Check actual product labels, preparation requirements and cross-contact for your needs.

Privacy questions

Read the privacy policy here or offline in Settings. For questions or requests about feedback held by the developer, use TestFlight's Send Beta Feedback option.
