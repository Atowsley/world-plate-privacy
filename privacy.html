Privacy

Recipes on your device.

Effective 5 October 2026. This policy is also available offline in World Plate Settings.

About World Plate

World Plate is published by Anthony Towsley. No app account is required. Recipes, both measurement versions, country introductions and offline atlas geometry are included in the app. World Plate has no recipe backend, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or tracking. Recipe drafts are awaiting kitchen testing and dietary review.

Data on your device

Favorites, measurement choice and appearance are saved in the app's local storage. Seven routine backup snapshots are retained, and recovery copies may be kept when restoring data. Your device or iCloud backups may include app data according to your system settings. World Plate does not upload these preferences to a publisher server.

Maps and external links

Apple map imagery uses Apple's network services and privacy practices. World Plate does not request your current location, Contacts or address book. The offline atlas geometry is bundled. Opening optional source links or this policy's website connects to the chosen provider, which can receive network and device information under its own privacy policy.

Sharing and exported backups

Sharing a recipe or exporting a backup uses the destination you choose in the system share or file interface. A backup contains favorite recipe identifiers and preferences. Your selected service may store that information under its own rules. World Plate does not choose recipients or upload your address book.

TestFlight feedback

Apple processes TestFlight usage, crash diagnostics and feedback under its privacy practices and may provide beta information to the developer. Feedback you send may include comments, screenshots and contact details you provide. We use feedback to respond and fix problems, and retain it while needed for those purposes. You can request deletion of feedback held by the developer through TestFlight's Send Beta Feedback option; Apple controls its own records.

Managing and deleting data

You can change preferences and remove favorites in the app. Older local backups can retain previous selections. Deleting the app removes its local app data; restoring a device backup may bring that data back. Delete exported files and device or cloud backups separately through the storage provider's controls. Recovery copies remain local until the app's data is removed.

Website and contact

The public policy and support website are hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitor IP addresses for security. The website adds no analytics, cookies, forms or tracking scripts. For support or privacy questions, open TestFlight, select World Plate and choose Send Beta Feedback. Policy updates will be dated and included in the app and on the website.
